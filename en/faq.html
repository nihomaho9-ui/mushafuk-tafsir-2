What is t2.mushafuk.online?

This address is the Mushafuk site for five additional Arabic tafsirs. Each explained verse has its own page.

How many books are here?

There are 5 tafsir books, all in Arabic, read verse by verse.

How many pages are here?

These five books have 4,564 published pages. The number is the count of verse pages that carry text. A book that does not comment on every verse is shorter.

The books and their pages

Each figure is the number of pages published for that book.

  • Tafsir Mujahid — 1,636
  • Tafsir Abd al-Razzaq — 2,112
  • Ibn Taymiyyah, Daqa'iq al-Tafsir — 312
  • Ibn Rajab, Rawa'i al-Tafsir — 268
  • Tafsir Ibn al-Mundhir — 236

How do I open a page?

Choose a book, then a surah, then a verse. The address looks like /mujahid/2/255/: book, surah, verse.

How does this relate to the main site?

The mushaf is on mushafuk.online. The earlier Arabic tafsirs are on tafsir.mushafuk.online. This site adds the five books below those.

Is reading free?

Yes. There are no ads and no account.
